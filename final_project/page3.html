Journaling

Journaling will allow you to unburden yourself of your emotions, especially in times when it is hard to quiet your mind. In the privacy of your journal, through doodles, pictures, videos, artistic mediums, apps, or voice memos, you can explore new perceptions about your past experiences and, in the long run, better understand yourself.

Bullet Journal - track habits, moods, and mental health triggers
Gratitude Journal - note things you are grateful for
Reflective Journal - reflect on your day and past experiences
Goal Journal - record specific accomplishments and track your progress
Unsent Letters Journal - write letters to people
