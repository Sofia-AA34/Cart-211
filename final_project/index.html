Mental Health Matters - You Matter

Mental health challenges vary from one person to another, as factors like emotional skills, substance use and genetics can make you more susceptible to psychological problems. It is the state of your overall mental being, and because it is interdependent with physical health, it can affect how you maintain your relationships, handle social situations, perform at school or work, participate in typical daily activities, learn at a level expected for your age and intelligence, handle change and cope with stressful situations.

If you are going through a tough time, you can begin your healing journey right here, right now. Select the circle of your choice, and remember you're always in control of your life.
