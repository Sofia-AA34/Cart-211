Meditation

Meditation and mindful exercises will help you focus on your thoughts, your body and your surroundings. You can do it anywhere, with anyone and at any time, while standing, walking or lying down. It can be as simple as turning off your screens, taking deep breaths and being mindful of your five senses. This exercise will allow you to anchor your attention on your breath rather than on any negative thoughts.

Meditation techniques you can try

4 - 7 - 8

Inhale through your nose for four seconds
Hold for seven seconds
Exhale completely through your mouth for eight seconds
Repeat four to eight times

Box Breathing

Breathe in through your nose for four seconds
Hold for four seconds
Breath out of your mouth or nose for four seconds
Pause with empty lungs before taking another breath for four seconds
Repeat four times or more
